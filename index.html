About

I label and quality-check the datasets that computer vision and language models actually learn from. At Niqo Robotics I annotate agricultural imagery for crop-detection and spray-alignment models — bounding boxes, polygons, and semantic segmentation at scale, checked with FiftyOne and refined through CVAT. Before that, at Mercedes-Benz R&D, I labeled datasets for face recognition, driver monitoring, and gesture recognition systems. I care about the boring-sounding part everyone skips: getting the label exactly right, every time, so the model downstream doesn't inherit my mistakes.
